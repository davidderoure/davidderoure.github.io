David De Roure

Bassist & researcher, Oxford — AI, live improvisation, computational music

David is Professor of e-Research at the University of Oxford, and plays bass. This page collects his music and creative-computing projects, where those two things meet. For his academic bio, research groups, and full publication list, see his University of Oxford profile.

Music & Creative Computing

Wolfson

A live jazz improvisation system: an LSTM model, trained on the Weimar Jazz Database, that listens to live bass and trades phrases back on saxophone in real time. Premiered as README at Wolfson College, Oxford, May 2026.

combo

A successor to Wolfson: a self-playing, multi-instrument improvising ensemble (bass, sax, keys, drums) built around a song/form data model and a mutual gesture-cueing layer, ported from AGRP, so any voice — human or AI — can prompt a handover or response from any other using a small vocabulary of playable gestures. Generalises Wolfson's one-human/one-AI trading to N voices, and asks whether the ensemble can develop recurring "tunes" of its own. Design and early implementation in progress.

Interval consonance & Euler's Gradus

A paper proposing an asymmetric formula for interval consonance and relating it to harmonic coincidence, building on Euler's Gradus suavitatis. Two companion integer sequences are catalogued in OEIS.

arXiv:2606.16412 OEIS A397104 OEIS A397106

AGRP — Audio Gesture Recognition Prototype

A real-time gesture recognizer built from pitch-tracked MIDI, written for PRiSM (Royal Northern College of Music) to a gesture vocabulary specified by George E. Lewis for use with his Voyager system. Concert-tested, 2022.

github.com/davidderoure/AGRP

Numbers into Notes

An interactive browser demo built for the Ada Lovelace Symposium (Oxford, 2015): number sequences — primes, Fibonacci, OEIS entries, and more — are converted into notes, played and notated live. The original hosted demo is no longer online, but the code still runs standalone and continues to be used for talks and demos.
